Return & Refund Policy

How refunds and cancellations work for MetaRoom3D subscriptions

Last Updated: August 31, 2026

MetaRoom3D sells downloadable desktop software on a subscription basis. Because our products are digital and delivered instantly, this policy explains exactly when you can get your money back, how to cancel, and what happens to your access afterwards. It applies to every purchase made through https://metaroom3d.com.

In short: try before you buy with our free trial, cancel any time in one click, and if a paid subscription genuinely does not work for you, contact us within 14 days of the charge for a full refund.

1. Free Trial

Our Personal edition includes a 7-day free trial. No payment is taken during the trial and it does not convert into a paid subscription automatically. We strongly encourage you to use the trial to confirm the software runs correctly on your hardware before purchasing.

2. Refund Window

You may request a full refund of any subscription payment within 14 days of the date that payment was charged. This applies to your first payment and to each renewal payment.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. We do not offer store credit or partial-period credit in place of a refund.

3. What Is Covered

We will refund a payment within the 14-day window in circumstances including:

  • The software does not run, install, or activate on a supported system
  • A defect prevents you from using a feature described on our product pages
  • You were charged for a renewal you intended to cancel
  • You were charged more than once for the same subscription period
  • You simply decided the product is not right for you

4. What Is Not Covered

We are unable to refund:

  • Requests made more than 14 days after the payment date
  • Subscription periods that have already been used in full and then renewed without a cancellation request
  • Purchases where the account has been used in breach of our Terms & Conditions
  • Third-party costs you incurred separately, such as hardware, cloud rendering, or camera equipment

Because the product is a digital download rather than a physical good, there is nothing to return or ship back to us. Cancelling your subscription is the equivalent of a return.

5. Cancelling Your Subscription

You can cancel at any time, and cancellation is always free. There are no cancellation fees and no minimum contract term.

  • From your PayPal account — open your automatic payments settings and cancel the MetaRoom3D billing agreement.
  • By contacting us — email the address at the bottom of this page and we will cancel it for you.

When you cancel, your subscription remains active until the end of the billing period you have already paid for. You are not charged again after that. Cancelling on its own does not generate a refund of the current period — if you also want that period refunded and you are inside the 14-day window, tell us so in your message.

6. Failed and Repeated Payments

If a renewal payment fails, our payment provider retries it up to three times before the subscription is suspended. You will not lose access during the retry period. If you believe a failed or duplicate charge has been taken in error, contact us and we will investigate and refund any incorrect charge in full, regardless of the 14-day window.

7. Price Changes

If we change the price of a plan, existing subscribers continue paying their original rate for as long as their subscription remains active and uninterrupted. Any price change affecting an existing subscription is notified in advance through our payment provider, and you may cancel before it takes effect.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email us at the address below with:

  • The email address used at checkout
  • The subscription or transaction reference from your payment receipt
  • A short note on what went wrong, if anything did

We aim to acknowledge every refund request within 2 business days and to process approved refunds within 7 business days. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take a further 5 to 10 business days for the money to appear on your statement.

9. Currency

All subscriptions are priced and charged in US Dollars (USD). Refunds are issued in USD for the amount originally charged. If your card or account is held in another currency, the amount you receive back may differ slightly from the amount you paid because of exchange rate movement between the two dates. We are unable to compensate for exchange rate differences or for any foreign transaction fee applied by your own bank.
