Our Story

We build the tools that turn real rooms into accurate 3D tours

MetaRoom3D is a small, product-first team building the software behind professional 3D virtual tours — from panorama capture all the way to a walkable, ±1cm-accurate dollhouse model.

MetaRoom3D started with a simple frustration: turning a set of 360° panoramas into an accurate, walkable 3D floor plan meant either an expensive proprietary pipeline or hours of manual measuring. We wanted a toolset that gets you to an architecturally accurate model without giving up control over the details.

That became the Room Layout Editor — a manual-first 3D layout tool where every automation is optional, never mandatory. From there we built Sphere, our chrome-ball HDRI stitcher, to solve the other half of the problem: capturing scene-accurate lighting without a full VFX retouch pass.

Today MetaRoom3D serves real estate agents, property developers, architects, and individual sellers who need their spaces to look — and measure — exactly as they are.

What We Believe

  • Manual always works. Automation should save time, not replace control — nothing is ever fully locked behind a paywall.
  • Accuracy is non-negotiable. ±1cm floor plans, scene-linear color pipelines, and industry-standard exports.
  • Open standards over lock-in. OpenEXR, ACES, OCIO, and DNG — built on formats the rest of the industry already trusts.
