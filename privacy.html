🔒 Privacy Policy

How MetaRoom3D collects, uses, and protects your information

Last Updated: May 5, 2026

Welcome to MetaRoom3D ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at https://metaroom3d.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our site.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you submit a property listing through our submission form, we may collect:

  • Your name or broker name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Property details (name, location, price, description)
  • Any files or content you submit to us

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit MetaRoom3D, certain information is collected automatically, including:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or link
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

This information is collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies, including those set by Google Analytics and Google AdSense (described below).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and publish property submissions you send us
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Improve and optimize the content and performance of our website
  • Understand how visitors interact with our site using analytics
  • Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

3. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (tracking ID: G-KNJFPMVYQR) to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics collects data such as pages visited, session duration, traffic sources, and device information using cookies.

This data is aggregated and anonymized — we cannot identify you personally from Google Analytics reports.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Google AdSense & Advertising

MetaRoom3D uses Google AdSense (publisher ID: ca-pub-4983613090498651) to display advertisements on our website. Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website and other websites on the internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

  • Google may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect data for ad personalization.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website.
  • Users can opt out of personalized advertising at aboutads.info or networkadvertising.org.

For more information on how Google uses data from sites that use its advertising services, visit: How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

5. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. MetaRoom3D uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies — Used by Google Analytics to track website usage
  • Advertising cookies — Used by Google AdSense to display relevant ads
  • Functional cookies — Used to remember your preferences during a session

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of our website. For guidance, visit allaboutcookies.org.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website integrates with several third-party services, each with their own privacy practices:

  • Web3Forms — Used to process our property submission form. See Web3Forms Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube — Used to embed tutorial videos. See Google/YouTube Privacy Policy.
  • Google Drive — Used to host downloadable files. See Google Privacy Policy.

We do not control the data practices of these third-party services and encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your consent — For example, publishing your broker contact details on a property listing as you requested
  • Service providers — Third-party tools (listed above) that help us operate our website
  • Legal requirements — If required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • Business transfers — In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

8. Data Retention

We retain personal information submitted through our property submission form for as long as the property listing remains active on our website, or as required by applicable law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

9. Children's Privacy

MetaRoom3D is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt out of personalized advertising
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policy of every website you visit.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed.
