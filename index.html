Sphere turns bracketed chrome-ball photographs into scene-accurate HDRI environment maps — HDR merge, spherical projection, seam correction, and tripod removal, without the manual retouch pass that the classic VFX workflow requires.

Load camera raw (DNG) and standard HDR formats straight in, merge your exposure brackets, and export through a scene-linear, color-managed pipeline built for real production work.

8-bit vs. 32-bit OpenEXR — same exposure move

Same shot, same exposure slider. The 8-bit render clips once it blows out or crushes to black — that detail is gone for good. The 32-bit OpenEXR keeps the full dynamic range, so highlights and shadows stay recoverable no matter how far you push exposure.
