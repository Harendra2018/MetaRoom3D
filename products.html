MetaRoom3D Sphere

Chrome-Ball HDRI Stitcher

Turns bracketed chrome-ball photographs into scene-accurate HDRI environment maps — HDR merge, spherical projection, seam correction, and tripod removal, without the manual retouch pass that the classic VFX workflow requires.

Sphere is the same stitcher on every plan — what changes is the export quality and color pipeline available to you. Every plan can merge, project, and correct a chrome-ball shoot; higher plans unlock larger exports and a scene-linear, color-managed pipeline for VFX-grade work. Personal, Professional, and Studio are single-seat licenses; Business and above support multiple seats on one license.
